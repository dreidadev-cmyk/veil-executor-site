Privacy Policy

What we collect

When you log in with Discord, we receive your user ID, username, and avatar. We store a session cookie to keep you logged in. We do not collect your email, IP address (beyond transient request logs at our CDN), or any payment information.

What we don't do

We do not sell your data. We do not embed third-party advertising trackers. We do not share your information with anyone except as required to provide the service (e.g., Discord for login).

Downloads

Downloading the Software does not require an account and does not associate your identity with the download.

Cookies

We use a single session cookie for authentication. No tracking cookies.

Your rights

You may delete your account at any time by contacting us on Discord. We will remove your stored data within 30 days.

Contact
